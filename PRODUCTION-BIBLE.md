# Gen X Guide to AI - Production Bible

## Media format rule - LOCKED

- The master episode format is **16:9 landscape**.
- The website must use the standard YouTube **watch/embed** presentation for the published episode video ID, not the `/shorts/` presentation, so the intended 16:9 framing is preserved.
- YouTube Shorts and other vertical/social placements are **separate 9:16 derivatives** of the master.
- Vertical derivatives must be deliberately reframed/cropped during production. Do not rely on a platform to auto-crop the 16:9 master.
- The eventual automated production pipeline must treat the 16:9 master and 9:16 social derivatives as separate controlled outputs.

## Website typography language

- Major editorial section headings use the strong Barlow Condensed treatment established in v3.
- Handwritten/marker typography is reserved for James + GPT asides, humorous commentary, and scribbled interventions.
- Social platform labels stay structured; the humorous comments beneath them use the handwritten treatment.
- This contrast is intentional: editorial type is what the programme/site says; handwriting is what James + GPT say about it.
