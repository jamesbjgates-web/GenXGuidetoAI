THE BORING BUT IMPORTANT BIT

PRIVACY

Gen X Guide to AI keeps this fairly simple. We only want the information we actually need to run the site and respond when you contact us.

When you email us

If you contact hello@genxguidetoai.com, we receive the email address you use and whatever information you choose to include in your message. We use that information to read and respond to you and, where relevant, to continue the conversation you started.

Technical information

Like most websites, our hosting provider may create routine server logs containing technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested pages, timestamps and error information. These logs are used for security, reliability and troubleshooting.

Links to other services

The site links to services such as YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Threads and X. Once you follow one of those links, that service's own privacy rules apply.

Cookies and tracking

We do not intentionally add advertising-tracking cookies through this site. Third-party services you choose to visit may use their own cookies or tracking technologies.

Your information

If you have a privacy question, or want us to correct or delete information you have sent us, email hello@genxguidetoai.com.

Plain English version: we are not interested in building a creepy dossier on you. If you email us, we need enough information to answer you. That's about the size of it.

Cookies and similar technologies

The current Gen X Guide to AI site does not intentionally deploy non-essential analytics or advertising cookies in its own page code. Ordinary technical infrastructure used by our hosting provider may still use strictly necessary technologies to deliver or secure the site. If we add analytics, advertising or other non-essential tracking later, we will update this notice and introduce appropriate consent controls before using them where required.

Links to third-party sites and social platforms take you away from Gen X Guide to AI. Those services have their own privacy and cookie practices.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to hello@genxguidetoai.com.
